## An Instance of LLMs use in a Scientific Research Context

*Talk, Oniris - Journée de la recherche, 2024*

This talk covers two fairly distinct topics:

- **Prompting**, a crucial element for getting the answers you want from LLMs, with a practical case and an example of a social media post.
- **Local LLMs**, with pointers to technologies (such as [Ollama](https://ollama.com/) and [Open WebUI](https://openwebui.com/)) and tutorials for those who want to install one themselves.
